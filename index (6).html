About Us

A club built on action, not just meetings.

The Interact Club of Stamford College Kandy is a student-led service organisation sponsored by a local Rotary club. Our members plan and run projects that serve our school, our community, and beyond — while picking up the kind of leadership experience a classroom alone can't teach.

"Interact" stands for international action, and that's the spirit we try to bring to every project: think locally, act with a wider world in mind. Membership is open to students who want to do more than attend meetings — we're looking for people who want to organise, show up, and follow through.

What We Work On

Five avenues, one motto.

Every Interact project falls under one of Rotary's areas of service. Here's how we put them into practice at Stamford.

01

Community Service

Local clean-ups, school-supply drives, and visits to elders' homes and children's shelters around Kandy.

02

International Service

Pen-pal and project exchanges with Interact clubs abroad, building friendships across borders.

03

Vocational Service

Career talks, skills workshops, and mentoring that help members think ahead about life after school.

04

Club Service

Keeping the club itself healthy — inducting new members, running elections, and planning the year ahead.
